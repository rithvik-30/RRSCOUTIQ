Three Pillars of Pressure Intelligence

How RR ScoutIQ transforms raw ball-by-ball delivery feeds into robust, reproducible Bayesian scouting ranks.

Read Methodology →
Step 01

Pre-Delivery Leverage Reconstruction

Every delivery is evaluated using a strict shift-1 state calculation (required run rate, balls remaining, wickets in hand, and phase par) prior to ball release, eliminating outcome leakage.

Step 02

Crossed-Effects Empirical Bayes Shrinkage

Leave-player-and-matches-out (LPMO) benchmarking isolates player skill from pitch conditions, ground geometry, and match-level random variance without contaminating expectation.

Step 03

Conservative Posterior Credible Bounds

Rankings penalize estimation variance using Z = 1.645 Gaussian credible bounds (LB90 for batters, UB90 for bowlers) to protect against small-sample noise and selection bias.

⚠️ Methodological Note: Point Estimates vs. Uncertainty-Adjusted Rankings

Official bowler ranking is determined strictly by ascending UB90 (95% one-sided upper credible bound), penalizing estimation variance to ensure sample reliability. Consequently, rankings can differ from raw posterior point estimates (ΔEB). For instance, Wanindu Hasaranga holds a more negative point estimate (-0.219 RPO) than Axar Patel (-0.150 RPO); however, Axar Patel's substantially larger volume (2,484 balls / 414 overs vs. 1,789 balls) produces lower latent standard error (0.4868 vs. 0.5293 RPO), yielding a superior conservative upper bound (+0.651 vs. +0.652 RPO). Axar Patel is therefore ranked #5 and Hasaranga #6. Rankings must not be reordered by raw point estimates.

Methodology & Mathematical Framework

Pre-delivery match-state reconstruction, Dynamic Leverage Index (DLI), crossed-effects empirical Bayes shrinkage, and Bayesian posterior credible bounds.

01

Dataset Provenance & Scope Coverage

The canonical dataset comprises 776 multi-format fixtures (367 IPL, 408 T20I, 1 Regional). After removing 32 rain-interrupted/curtailed fixtures, the clean legal modeling population contains exactly 744 matches and 168,104 clean legal deliveries.

Clean Population = 362 IPL (48.66%) + 381 T20I (51.21%) + 1 Regional (0.13%)
Clean Legal Balls = 168,104 | Excluded Rain Deliveries = 4,161 (32 matches)
02

Dynamic Leverage Index (DLI)

DLI is the approved contextual measure of delivery-level match pressure. In chases, it evaluates required run rate relative to phase scoring par; in first innings, it grows with time progression $\Phi(t)$ and run-rate stagnation.

DLI_chase = clamp[(RRR/CRR_phase) × (10/(W+1))^0.50 × (1 + 24/(B+12))^0.50, 0.10, 5.00]
DLI_set = clamp[Φ(t) × (10/(W+1))^0.60 × (1 + max(0, (CRR_phase - CRR)/6))^0.50, 0.20, 3.50]
where Φ(t) = 0.80 + 0.70 × (t/120)^1.20; Super Over DLI = 3.50
03

Pre-Delivery Match-State Reconstruction

Every legal delivery is modeled within a strict shift-1 sequence. All features—balls remaining, wickets in hand, score, required run rate, and phase par—are evaluated strictly prior to the bowler releasing the ball, guaranteeing zero outcome leakage.

State(t) = f({d_1, d_2, ... d_{t-1}}) | Outcome(t) strictly excluded
04

Empirical Bayes Crossed-Effects Shrinkage

Leave-player-and-matches-out (LPMO) benchmarking ensures that when evaluating player $i$, all fixtures involving player $i$ are excluded from baseline estimation. Crossed-effects modeling partitions observed variance between talent, match environment, and noise.

Var(Obs_i) = tau_player^2 + sigma_match^2 + sigma_noise^2
B_i = tau^2 / (tau^2 + SE_i^2) | Δ_EB,i = B_i × Δ_raw,i
05

Batter Ranking: 95% One-Sided Lower Credible Bound

Batters are ranked strictly in descending order of the 95% one-sided lower posterior credible bound ($LB90$) for contextual batting value-add. A candidate establishes confirmed positive value-add only when $LB90 > 0$.

LB90 = Δ_EB - 1.645 × se_latent (descending order; higher is superior)
Threshold: Confirmed Positive if LB90 > 0 (achieved by Ranks 1–3 only)
06

Bowler Ranking: 95% One-Sided Upper Credible Bound

Bowlers are ranked in ascending order of the 95% one-sided upper posterior credible bound ($UB90$) for contextual economy delta. This bound defines a conservative ceiling on expected run concession under pressure. Ranking by ascending upper bound prioritizes bowlers who minimize credible run leakage.

UB90 = Δ_EB + 1.645 × se_latent (ascending order; lower is superior)
Key Rule: Point estimates and uncertainty bounds can produce different orders (e.g. Axar #5 vs Hasaranga #6)
07

Statistical Uncertainty & Z = 1.645 Multiplier

Under the Gaussian posterior approximation, the endpoint of a 90% two-sided credible interval corresponds mathematically to a 95% one-sided posterior credible bound. The multiplier $Z = 1.645$ directly guards against small-sample noise.

P(Δ > LB90 | Data) = 0.95 (Batters) | P(Δ < UB90 | Data) = 0.95 (Bowlers)
Multiplier Z = 1.645 (90% two-sided CI endpoint / 95% one-sided bound)
08

Cohort Eligibility Criteria

Candidates are evaluated against three objective qualification gates prior to ranking: minimum 500 career legal balls, minimum 100 pressure deliveries ($\text{DLI} \ge 0.90$), and an epistemic standard error threshold.

Batters: Career Balls ≥ 500 • Pressure Balls ≥ 100 • SE ≤ 0.035 (106 eligible)
Bowlers: Career Balls ≥ 500 • Pressure Balls ≥ 100 • SE ≤ 0.65 RPO (109 eligible)
09

Methodological Limitations & Epistemic Boundaries

RR ScoutIQ produces descriptive contextual rankings rather than deterministic future predictions. Deliveries with missing or zero exposure (e.g. Tier 4 balls for certain top-order batters) are never imputed. Player ratings reflect historical contextual delivery execution under empirical Bayes shrinkage.

Zero Imputation: No synthesis of unobserved match states | No causal attribution
